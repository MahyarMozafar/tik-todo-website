Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Tik is simple, but it has everything you need to keep your day in order.

Today

What's due today and anything that's late, with a progress bar. Done tasks slide to the bottom, and the last one of the day gets a burst of confetti.

Quick add

The glass + button melts into a text field and stays open after each task, so you can type a whole day in one go.

Lists

An Inbox and your own lists with a color and an icon, plus smart lists: Today, Scheduled, All and Completed.

Task details

Notes, subtasks, a day and a time, priority, a photo, and repeats: every day, certain weekdays, every few days, every week, month or year.

Reminders

Notifications with Mark as Done and Remind Me in 10 Minutes buttons that work without opening the app.

Widgets

Today's tasks on your Home Screen. Tick them right there.

English and Farsi

Switch the language inside the app, whatever your phone's language is. Farsi is fully right-to-left, with the Vazirmatn font.

Shamsi calendar

Shamsi is the default and weeks start on Saturday. The Gregorian calendar is one tap away.

Make it yours

Light or dark, 11 colors, 6 app icons, haptics and sounds. Turn off any task detail you don't use.

Glass

The bars and buttons are made of glass: whatever scrolls behind them softly blurs.

Phones and tablets

A floating tab bar on phones, and a sidebar with search on iPad and tablets.

Private

No account and no internet. Your tasks stay on your phone.

FAQ

Questions and answers

Is Tik free?

Yes, completely. No ads, no account and nothing to buy inside the app.

Where are my tasks saved?

Only on your phone. Tik never connects to the internet; the Android app doesn't even have permission to use it.

If your phone's own backup (Google or iCloud) is on, it may back up Tik's tasks too, like it does for other apps.

Does it need the internet?

No. Tik works fully offline. You only need the internet to download it.

Why isn't it on Google Play or the App Store?

Tik is a free, personal project and isn't in the stores yet. Download the Android app from this page, and install the iPhone app for free with Xcode.

Android says "unknown app". Is it safe?

Android shows this for every app that doesn't come from Google Play. Tik's file is the same file as its official release on GitHub, and its code is open for anyone to read.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 8 or newer, on phones and tablets. iPhone and iPad with iOS 27.

How do I update it?

Android: download the new file from this page and install it. It installs over the old one, and your tasks stay.

iPhone: get the new code from GitHub and press Run in Xcode again.

Install on iPhone for free

You need a Mac with Xcode 27 and a free Apple ID. The paid Apple Developer Program is not needed.

  1. Install Xcode 27 on your Mac, clone Tik's repo from GitHub, and open Tik.xcodeproj.
  2. In Xcode, go to Settings → Accounts and sign in with your Apple ID.
  3. Select the Tik target, open Signing & Capabilities, and pick your Personal Team. Do the same for the TikWidget target.
  4. Plug in your iPhone, turn on Developer Mode (Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode), pick your phone at the top of Xcode, and press Run.
  5. The first time, trust the app on the phone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.

With a free account the app runs for 7 days. After that, plug the phone in and press Run again; your tasks stay where they are.

If Xcode complains about the bundle ID or the App Group, change com.mahyarmozafar in project.yml to something of your own and run xcodegen.
